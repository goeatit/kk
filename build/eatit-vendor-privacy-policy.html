PRIVACY POLICY – Eatit Partner (Vendor App)

Last Updated: 10/02/2026

Introduction

Eatit LLP ("Eatit", "we", "our", "us") respects the privacy of our restaurant partners ("Vendors", "Partners").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information when you use the Eatit Partner mobile application.

By registering or using the Eatit Partner app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Business & Identity Information (KYC Details)

  • Restaurant name and registered business address
  • Owner / Manager name
  • Mobile number and email address
  • Login credentials and authentication details
  • FSSAI Certificate number and uploaded certificate copy
  • PAN Card number and uploaded PAN copy
  • GST Certificate and GST number (if provided)
  • Cancelled cheque copy (if provided)
  • Any other business verification documents required for compliance

b) Banking & Payment Information

  • Account holder name
  • Bank name
  • Branch name (if required)
  • Account number
  • Re-entered account number (for verification)
  • Account type
  • IFSC code

This information is collected solely for payment settlement, vendor verification, fraud prevention, and regulatory compliance.

c) Operational & Usage Information

  • Orders received and fulfilled
  • Menu items, pricing, and availability
  • Slot booking and preparation status
  • App interaction data and feature usage logs

d) Location Information

  • Location access is collected only with your explicit permission
  • Used for displaying restaurant location, verifying service availability, and improving order discovery accuracy
  • Location is not tracked in the background without consent

e) Files, Images & Media

Vendors may upload:

  • Menu images
  • Restaurant photos
  • Business documents (FSSAI, PAN, GST, Cancelled Cheque, etc.)

Files are uploaded only through explicit vendor action and are used solely for verification, compliance, and restaurant profile management.

f) SMS Information

  • SMS access is used only for automatic OTP verification during login or registration
  • Eatit does not read, store, or access personal SMS content
  • No promotional or personal messages are accessed

g) Device & Technical Information

  • Device model and operating system version
  • Network type and connectivity status
  • App crash logs and performance analytics

Used for security, fraud detection, and performance improvement.

h) Notifications

  • New order alerts
  • Order status updates
  • Payment or settlement notifications
  • System or compliance alerts

Push notifications can be disabled from device settings.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

We collect vendor information to:

  • Verify business authenticity and regulatory compliance
  • Process vendor onboarding (KYC verification)
  • Enable secure bank settlements
  • Display restaurant listings and menus to customers
  • Process and manage food orders
  • Send operational alerts and notifications
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, or unauthorized activities
  • Comply with applicable laws including IT Act 2000, tax laws, and food safety regulations

3. Sharing of Information

Vendor information may be shared with:

  • Customers (limited to restaurant name, menu, and order-related details)
  • Payment gateway and banking partners for settlement processing
  • Cloud hosting and analytics service providers
  • Government or regulatory authorities when legally required

Eatit does not sell, rent, or trade vendor data to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/SSL)
  • Secure authentication and access controls
  • Restricted internal access to sensitive information
  • Secure cloud storage infrastructure
  • Regular system monitoring and vulnerability checks

While we take strong precautions, no system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

Vendor data is retained:

  • For as long as the vendor account remains active
  • As required under applicable tax, financial, or regulatory laws
  • For fraud prevention and audit purposes

Vendors may request account deletion subject to legal retention requirements.

6. Vendor Rights

Vendors have the right to:

  • Access and review their information
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of account (subject to legal obligations)
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

Requests can be submitted to: support@eatitgo.com

7. Account Suspension or Termination

Eatit may suspend or terminate vendor accounts in cases of:

  • Fraudulent documentation
  • Invalid or misleading banking information
  • Repeated order cancellations or service violations
  • Legal or regulatory non-compliance
  • Misuse of the platform

8. Children's Privacy

The Eatit Partner application is strictly intended for business use.

It is not designed for individuals under 18 years of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.

Updated versions will be published within the app or on the official website.

Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
