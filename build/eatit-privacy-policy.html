PRIVACY POLICY – Eatit LLP

Last Updated: 7 February 2026

Eatit LLP ("Eatit", "we", "our", or "us") values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect your information when you use the Eatit mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By using Eatit, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Profile details
  • Feedback and customer support communications

b) Usage Information

  • App interactions and browsing activity
  • Order history
  • Restaurant preferences
  • Device information (device model, OS version, IP address, app version)

c) Location Information

With your explicit permission, we collect location data to recommend nearby restaurants and enable accurate delivery services. You may disable location access at any time via your device settings.

d) Payment Information

Payments are processed securely through third-party payment gateways. Eatit does NOT store debit/credit card numbers, CVV details, UPI IDs, or banking passwords. All payment processing is handled by PCI-DSS compliant providers.

e) Communication & Notification Data

We may send push notifications related to order updates, delivery status, offers, and important account information. Users can disable push notifications at any time from their device settings.

f) SMS Information

SMS access is used only for automatic OTP verification during login or registration. Eatit does not read, store, or share personal SMS messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display nearby restaurants and offers
  • Process and deliver orders
  • Send order confirmations and updates
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve app functionality and performance
  • Detect and prevent fraud or misuse
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3. Sharing of Information

We share user information only when necessary with:

  • Restaurant partners (order-related details only)
  • Delivery partners (name, address, contact details)
  • Payment gateways and financial service providers
  • SMS/email service providers
  • Legal authorities when required by law

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

4. Third-Party Services

Eatit uses trusted third-party services to provide core app functionality, including:

  • Google Maps & Google Places (location and restaurant discovery)
  • Firebase (notifications, analytics, crash reporting)
  • Facebook Login (authentication)
  • Razorpay (secure payment processing)

These services may collect information as governed by their respective privacy policies. Eatit does not control or store third-party collected data.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies or similar technologies within the app or website for login functionality, analytics, and improving user experience. Users can manage cookie preferences via browser or device settings.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures including encrypted connections, secure cloud infrastructure, access controls, PCI-DSS compliant payment processing, and periodic security reviews. However, no system is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request:

  • Access to their personal data
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of their account
  • Withdrawal of consent

To exercise these rights, contact: support@eatitgo.com

8. Children's Privacy

Eatit does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If such data is discovered, we will delete it promptly.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal data as long as your account remains active or as required for legal, tax, regulatory, fraud prevention, or operational purposes. Users may request account deletion at any time.

10. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites or restaurant pages. Eatit is not responsible for their privacy practices.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted within the app or website with a revised date.
